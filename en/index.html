Privacy Policy for KVP Launcher / Interface for the Elderly (Android App)

This privacy policy describes how our application processes, uses, and protects user information. We created KVP Launcher as free software designed to simplify smartphone use for older adults and visually impaired individuals. Our main priority is your privacy and security.

Data Collection and Use (App Permissions)

Our application is completely autonomous (works locally) and requires access to certain device functions solely to ensure the basic convenience of the launcher:

  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): Used exclusively to read the phone book in order to select and display contacts in the app's speed dial menu for your convenience. This data is processed strictly locally on your device and is not transmitted to third parties or external servers.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used exclusively to operate the system flashlight function (controlling the camera flash). The application does not take photos, record videos, or transmit images.
  • Package Visibility (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Used exclusively to detect and display the full list of apps installed on the device, allowing the user to conveniently launch them from the main menu.
  • Notification Access (NotificationListenerService): Used only to intercept and display incoming notifications (e.g., missed calls or new messages) in an enlarged, easily readable dialog box. We do not read system logs or store your notification history.

2. Data Transfer to Third Parties

We do not collect, store, track, or transmit any of your personal data, message content, contact lists, or call history to external servers, advertising networks, or any third parties. All data operations occur entirely locally on your device. The app does not contain built-in analytics tools or hidden tracking mechanisms.

3. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app's functionality or security requirements. All changes take effect immediately upon their publication on this page.
